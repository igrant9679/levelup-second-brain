The job of a note isn't storage — it's to make a future thought cheaper. A note earns its keep by being connected, not by being filed.

Three rules

  • Every note links to at least one other. See On compounding knowledge.
  • Fleeting notes get a weekly trial; survivors get promoted to Evergreen.
  • Write the note so a stranger (future you) understands it with no context.
